# Employee Benefits

Kestrel Labs matches 401(k) contributions dollar for dollar up to 5 percent of base salary for employees in the United States. Matching contributions vest immediately.

Health, dental, and vision insurance begin on the first day of employment. The company pays 90 percent of employee premiums and 70 percent of premiums for dependents.

Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 8 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

Every employee receives an annual learning budget of 1,500 US dollars for courses, books, and conferences. Unused learning budget does not roll over.
