# Kestrel Labs Company Overview

Kestrel Labs is a fictional agricultural robotics company founded in 2016 in Boise, Idaho by Mara Okafor and Tobias Lindqvist. The company designs autonomous drones that survey crops, map irrigation problems, and apply targeted treatments.

Kestrel Labs employs roughly 340 people across three offices: Boise (headquarters), Rotterdam, and Brisbane. The Rotterdam office opened in 2020 and leads European sales. The Brisbane office opened in 2022 and runs the Asia-Pacific support team.

The company sells two drone lines: the K7 Surveyor, a mapping drone, and the K9 Applicator, a spraying drone. Software subscriptions to the FieldSight analytics platform account for about 45 percent of annual revenue.

The current chief executive officer is Mara Okafor. Tobias Lindqvist serves as chief technology officer.
