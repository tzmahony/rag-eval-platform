# Firmware Release Process

Drone firmware is released on a six-week train. Each release passes through three stages: internal flight testing, a beta ring, and general availability.

Internal flight testing lasts at least 10 days and must include a minimum of 50 logged flight hours across both drone lines.

The beta ring is opt-in and consists of about 120 customer farms. Firmware stays in the beta ring for 14 days. A release is halted if the beta ring reports any Severity 1 issue or if the crash rate exceeds 0.5 crashes per 1,000 flight hours.

Emergency safety patches may skip the beta ring with approval from the chief technology officer and the head of flight safety.

Customers can delay non-safety firmware updates for up to 60 days. Safety updates install automatically at the next power-on.
