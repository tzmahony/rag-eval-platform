# New Hire Onboarding

New hires receive their laptop by courier at least three business days before their start date. On day one, they attend a welcome session with the People team that starts at 9:30 in the morning local time.

Each new hire is paired with an onboarding buddy from another team for their first 90 days. Managers hold check-ins at 30, 60, and 90 days.

All new hires must complete security awareness training within their first 7 days. Engineers must also complete the drone safety course before entering the flight-test lab.

The probationary period lasts 90 days for all roles.
