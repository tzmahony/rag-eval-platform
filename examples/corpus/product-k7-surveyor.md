# K7 Surveyor Product Sheet

The K7 Surveyor is a fixed-wing mapping drone designed for large farms. It captures multispectral imagery that FieldSight converts into crop health maps.

Specifications:
- Maximum flight time: 95 minutes on a single battery.
- Coverage: up to 400 hectares per flight at 120 meters altitude.
- Camera: five-band multispectral sensor with a ground resolution of 3 centimeters per pixel.
- Weight: 4.2 kilograms including battery.
- Operating temperature: minus 10 to 45 degrees Celsius.

The K7 is launched by hand and lands on its belly, so it does not need a runway. It is rated to fly in sustained winds up to 45 kilometers per hour.

The list price of the K7 Surveyor is 18,500 US dollars, which includes one battery and a 12-month FieldSight Basic subscription.
