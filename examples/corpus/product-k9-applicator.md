# K9 Applicator Product Sheet

The K9 Applicator is a hexacopter that applies fertilizer and crop protection products to targeted zones identified in FieldSight.

Specifications:
- Tank capacity: 30 liters.
- Maximum flight time: 18 minutes with a full tank.
- Spray width: up to 7 meters.
- Weight: 26 kilograms empty, 56 kilograms at maximum takeoff weight.
- Obstacle avoidance: front and rear radar plus a downward lidar.

Because of its takeoff weight, operating the K9 in the United States requires an FAA Part 137 agricultural aircraft operator certificate in addition to a Part 107 remote pilot certificate.

The list price of the K9 Applicator is 32,000 US dollars. Spare batteries cost 1,150 US dollars each. A full charge of one battery takes 25 minutes with the Kestrel fast charger.
