# Paid Time Off Policy

Full-time employees accrue paid time off (PTO) at a rate of 1.75 days per month, which equals 21 days per year. Part-time employees accrue PTO in proportion to their scheduled hours.

Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused PTO days into the next calendar year. Any unused days above that limit are forfeited on January 1. Unused PTO is not paid out at the end of the year.

PTO requests of three days or longer must be submitted in the HR portal at least 14 days in advance and approved by the employee's direct manager. Requests shorter than three days require only 2 business days of notice.

After five years of continuous service, the accrual rate increases to 2.25 days per month.

Sick leave is tracked separately from PTO. Every employee receives 10 sick days per year, and sick days do not carry over.
