# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

Kestrel Labs follows a hybrid model. Employees assigned to an office are expected on site on Tuesdays and Thursdays, which are designated as anchor days. Other days may be worked remotely.

Hardware and flight-test engineers are exempt from the hybrid model and work on site four days per week because their work requires lab equipment.

Employees may work fully remotely from another country for up to 20 working days per calendar year. Longer stays abroad require approval from both HR and the legal team, because of tax and employment law obligations.

Remote employees receive a one-time home office stipend of 600 US dollars and a monthly internet stipend of 40 US dollars.
