# Information Security Policy

All employees must enable multi-factor authentication (MFA) on their Kestrel accounts. Hardware security keys are mandatory for engineers with production access; other staff may use an authenticator app.

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Passwords are not required to rotate on a schedule but must be changed immediately if a compromise is suspected.

Suspected security incidents, including lost laptops and phishing emails that were clicked, must be reported to the security team within 1 hour of discovery. Reports go to the #sec-incidents channel or to security@kestrel.example.

Customer flight data is classified as Confidential. Confidential data may not be stored on personal devices or in personal cloud accounts.

Laptops lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Full-disk encryption is enforced on every company laptop.
