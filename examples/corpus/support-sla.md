# Customer Support Service Levels

Kestrel offers three support tiers: Standard, Priority, and Enterprise.

Standard support is included with every purchase. It provides email support with a first response within 2 business days.

Priority support costs 3,000 US dollars per year. It adds phone support and a first response within 8 business hours.

Enterprise support is available to customers operating 20 or more drones. It includes a named technical account manager and a first response within 1 hour for Severity 1 issues, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

A Severity 1 issue is defined as a fleet-wide outage or a safety-related malfunction. The Brisbane team handles after-hours Severity 1 coverage for all regions.
