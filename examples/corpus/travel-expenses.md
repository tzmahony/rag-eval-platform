# Travel and Expense Policy

All business travel must be booked through the Kestrel travel portal, TripDesk. Bookings made outside TripDesk are reimbursed only with written approval from a vice president.

Economy class is required for flights under six hours. Employees may book premium economy for flights of six hours or longer. Business class requires approval from the chief financial officer.

The daily meal allowance is 75 US dollars for domestic travel and 95 US dollars for international travel. Alcohol is not reimbursable unless the expense is part of an approved client dinner.

Hotel stays are capped at 220 US dollars per night in most cities. In designated high-cost cities, including London, Tokyo, and San Francisco, the cap is 340 US dollars per night.

Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the end of the trip. Receipts are required for any single expense of 25 US dollars or more. Reports submitted after 60 days are not reimbursed.
