# Warranty and Returns

Every new K7 Surveyor and K9 Applicator includes a 24-month limited hardware warranty covering manufacturing defects. Batteries are covered for 12 months or 300 charge cycles, whichever comes first.

The warranty does not cover crash damage, water immersion, or damage caused by unauthorized modifications. Customers can buy KestrelCare, an extended protection plan that adds one accidental-damage replacement per year, for 2,400 US dollars per year for the K9 and 1,100 US dollars per year for the K7.

Unused products in their original packaging may be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Opened products returned within 30 days are subject to a 15 percent restocking fee.

To start a warranty claim, customers open a ticket in the support portal and receive a return merchandise authorization (RMA) number. Repairs are typically completed within 10 business days of the unit arriving at the service center.
